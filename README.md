01 drawing 

- utilised various shapes in order to create pizza outline
- experimented with rgb codes to choose colour of base/toppings
- the pi shape looked like cut pepperoni so was inspired by that 


02 movement 

- really loved the pi shape so wanted to discover new ways to utilise it
- looked on p5.js and found a guide on how to make a moving pac man 
- wanted to make the image more detailed so incorporated a variety of shapes including squares (to make the pellets) and circles/ellipses (to make the eyes), makes image feel more alive/cartoon-like  
- no reason for why he's purple. he just is!


03 multiples 

falling shapes 

- attempted to make falling snow but unfortunately couldn't wrap my head around it
- new idea: falling bricks 
- kept the original falling rectangle shapes but adjusted width, height and colour to match brick properties of the wall that they fall onto
- wall consists of many rectangles (shocking!), added a repetitive colour pattern so more interesting to look at


creeper 

- text is lyrics to a famous Minecraft parody of the song "DJ Got Us Falling In Love Again"
- numbers may initially confuse reader as they are not in a strict pattern (ascending/descending/etc.) but if they pay close attention they can notice the numbers match the amount of letters in each word, e.g 'so' would be 2
- picked the colour scheme to match the Minecraft creeper colour palette
- font + colour scheme make the reader feel as if they are hacking/decoding a secret message 


circle brush

abstract shape

dot brush 

- demonstrates "if" and "else" functions
- selected colour palette which is able to paint a tree (brown and green)
